The wake-up

Complacency isn't laziness.
It's drowsiness at the wheel.

Your calendar is full. The emails get answered. The checklist gets checked. But somewhere along a familiar road, the achiever slips into autopilot — relying on the same old methods to do the same old things, assuming the same old results. Rome did it. Richard Petty did it. Kodak, Blockbuster, and Pan Am did it. Historians even have a name for it: victory disease.

In the 1940s, engineers cut gashes into a New Jersey highway because drivers kept drifting into oncoming traffic. Those rumble strips now prevent 70% of drift accidents — a little sound along a familiar road that wakes the driver up. That's what this app is for: a rumble strip for your potential. Evaluate where you are, work the seven steps, and pull yourself back into the center lane of your purpose.
