# The Pitch — From Drift to Drive: The Companion App

*Working notes for presenting the app to Chris Robinson.*

---

## The one-liner

> **Imagine an app that isn't a repeat of your book — it's the driver's seat.**
> The book is the map. This puts readers' hands on the wheel: it diagnoses their
> drift, walks them through your seven steps with a working tool at every mile
> marker, and keeps them driving for thirty days after the last page.

## The 60-second version

Chris, every reader finishes your book fired up — and then life happens. The book
warns them about exactly that: complacency doesn't broadcast its presence. This
app is the rumble strip that keeps working after the final chapter.

- **It diagnoses.** The Drift Check turns your awareness questions into a
  two-minute assessment with a score, a zone — Driver's Seat, Cruise Control, or
  Drifting — and three personalized next moves. Readers see *their own* drift, in
  numbers, and then see it drop on the next check.
- **It makes the framework muscle memory.** Every step has a tool readers
  actually use: they build their I-Exam Chart, gather their ten BVACC resources
  (and get told to *stop* at ten), write today's index card, vet a mentor with
  your five questions, audit their rooms, divide $4,000 into ten clients into
  one phone call, and run the four-question debrief. Learn a little, do a little
  — engineered into software.
- **It keeps them driving.** The 30-Day Drive Challenge is a highway — thirty
  mile markers, their car sitting on today, one focused action at a time, streaks
  that reward "every day that ends with day." And when they finish all seven
  steps, the app doesn't say "done" — it starts Lap 2. The loop from your last
  chapter *is the product*.
- **It's their Garage.** One cockpit screen shows their score trend, their
  vision, their next easiest step, their streak. You can't expect what you won't
  inspect — so the app inspects.

## Why it's good for the business

- **List growth** — the Drift Check is a natural lead magnet feeding the
  official CAP quiz and Chris's email list.
- **Cohorts & coaching funnels** — 30-day challenge cohorts are a ready-made
  group-coaching product; Garage data shows readers exactly when they need a
  coach ("your score went up — get a trail angel").
- **Speaking** — audiences take the Drift Check live from the stage; the room
  sees its collective drift score in real time.
- **Zero friction today** — no app store, no accounts, no backend. It runs in
  any browser, deploys free, and everything readers enter stays private on their
  device.

## The roadmap (if he loves it)

1. Wire the email capture to his platform; connect the Drift Check to the CAP
   funnel.
2. Shareable drift-score graphic ("I'm in the Driver's Seat — where are you?").
3. Challenge cohorts with start dates, reminders, and a leaderboard.
4. Printable I-Exam Chart and index-card PDFs; audiobook chapter deep links.
5. Native wrapper (PWA) so it installs to the home screen with one tap.
